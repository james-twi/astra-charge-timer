Assumptions

Defaults are the Astra Electric: 51 kWh usable battery and ~90% wall-to-battery efficiency. Granny = 3-pin plug at 2.3 kW; Wallbox = the Astra's 7.4 kW onboard AC limit. Editing "Charger kW" switches to Custom. Start time is rounded down to the nearest 5 minutes, so you finish slightly early rather than late.
